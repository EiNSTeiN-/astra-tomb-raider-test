# Ice 002

The three unmodified 1K JPEG maps delivered as `ice-color.jpg`,
`ice-normal.jpg` and `ice-roughness.jpg` come from
[Ice 002 by ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ice002).
This is a procedurally generated material, distributed under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/),
as documented on the [ambientCG license page](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/).

The runtime shader changes their tint and scale for frozen meltwater in the
fictional mountain chapter. No texture pixels were edited. Archive URLs,
member names, byte lengths and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `sources.json`.
