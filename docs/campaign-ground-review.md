after the crystal wall detail milestone. It uses the visible explorer and the
normal follow camera at prepared legal dry standing positions. It broadens the
recent crystal work to the other chapter themes.

courts, two of the 18 discoveries per chapter, and the first and last path
midpoints. All 194 views were captured and reviewed on contact sheets, with
selected full-size inspections. Temporary progress opens objective gates and
field requirements; enemies are disabled and audio is muted. These are static
composition and rendering samples, not completed objective playthroughs.

Projected court-centre coordinates prove only that a point lies within the
camera frustum. They do not establish that the whole court is visible or free
from foreground occlusion. The close sky and eclipse wall views illustrate
this limit.
The camera also crops some tall structures while retaining its normal arm and
clear registered body targets. This does not alone establish a camera defect.

The visual sample does not cover all 144 discoveries, complete connecting
routes, underwater areas, moving mechanisms, climbing and raised traversal,
combat, subjective listening or consumer device performance. No whole-surface
or AAA graphics acceptance is claimed. The

jungle reading slabs and moves seven to side pockets. Its eight court comparison
states match this baseline; the original jungle findings above describe the

error, a legal grounded player root, full health, clear camera space and clear
