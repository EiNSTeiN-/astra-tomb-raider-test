# Aerial Beach 01

The three unmodified 2K JPEG maps delivered as `desert-sand-color.jpg`,
`desert-sand-normal.jpg` and `desert-sand-roughness.jpg` are from
[Aerial Beach 01 by Rob Tuytel](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_beach_01).
Poly Haven distributes this asset under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as
documented on its [asset license page](https://polyhaven.com/license).

The runtime shader changes their tint and scale for the fictional desert.
No texture pixels were edited. Delivery URLs, byte lengths and SHA-256 checksums
are recorded in `sources.json`.
