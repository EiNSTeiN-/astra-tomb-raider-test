# Rocks Ground 09

Author: Rob Tuytel. Source: [Poly Haven asset page](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocks_ground_09).

The asset is published under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The [Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) permits reuse and redistribution. Verified 2026-10-29.

The three 2K delivery JPEGs are recompressed from the supplied diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps, preserving dimensions. The source manifest records original and delivery hashes and encoding settings. The game supplies its charcoal tint, ash deposits and mineral staining in the terrain shader. No source-site reference render is redistributed.
